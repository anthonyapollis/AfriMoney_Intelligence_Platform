# AfriMoney Intelligence Platform

Snowflake, dbt and Snowpark ML analytics for African remittance corridors. The case study models five million synthetic transfers across 17 corridors, settlement operations, customer economics and governed risk intelligence.

## AfriMoney Corridor Intelligence — Qlik Sense portfolio app

![AfriMoney executive dashboard](qlik/portfolio/previews/afrimoney-corridor-intelligence-executive.png)

The six-page Qlik Sense Desktop report uses an original mobile-money visual language and a bottom navigation dock. It covers corridor performance, settlement/FX, channels and customers, fraud/credit/churn signals, plus methodology and model trust.

Download the ready-to-open QVF and bundled extension from [`qlik/portfolio`](qlik/portfolio/README.md).

## Portfolio evidence

- 5 million synthetic transfers across 17 corridors
- R10.329B sent and R8.061B delivered
- R863.3M net revenue
- Fraud AUC 0.9582, churn AUC 0.9905 and credit AUC 0.9475
- 500,000 SCD Type 2 customer records

All values are portfolio case-study evidence. Predictive metrics are presented with scope and limitations inside the Qlik app.
